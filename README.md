CS 255 DriverPass Project
Project Summary

The DriverPass project involved designing a system for a client who wanted to improve driver training and help students prepare for their driving tests. DriverPass wanted a cloud-based system where customers could create accounts, purchase training packages, take online classes and practice tests, and schedule driving lessons. The system also needed to support employees, instructors, management, and IT staff.

What I Did Well

I think I did particularly well at taking the requirements from the client and turning them into a system design. Creating the UML diagrams helped me break down the different parts of DriverPass and understand how users would interact with the system. I think the scheduling diagrams were especially helpful because they show how the system checks availability before creating a driving lesson.

What I Would Improve

If I could revise one part of my work, I would improve some of the technical details in my system design document. I would spend more time reviewing the relationships between different parts of the system and making sure each diagram communicates the process as clearly as possible.

Understanding the User's Needs

I interpreted the user's needs by identifying what DriverPass wanted customers and employees to accomplish with the system and then including those requirements in my design. For example, customers needed to be able to schedule driving lessons, so my design checks the customer's package and the availability of both an instructor and vehicle before confirming a lesson. Considering user needs is important because a system needs to solve the problems of the people who will actually use it, not just function correctly from a technical perspective.

My Approach to Software Design

My approach to designing software is to first understand the requirements and then break the system into smaller processes that are easier to understand. UML diagrams helped me visualize the users, processes, interactions, and classes involved in DriverPass. In the future, I would continue using requirements analysis, UML diagrams, and user feedback throughout the design process. I would also review and test the design throughout development so problems can be identified before they become more difficult to fix.
